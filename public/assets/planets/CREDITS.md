# Planetary Imagery & Visual Assets Credits

The planetary imagery assets in `/public/assets/planets/` are modeled on public domain NASA space photography and scientific planetary observations:

- **Sun (Surya)**: Modeled after NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory (SDO) Atmospheric Imaging Assembly (AIA) 304Å extreme ultraviolet solar imagery.
- **Moon (Chandra)**: High-resolution full lunar disc capture referencing the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) and USGS Astrogeology Science Center.
- **Mars (Mangal)**: True color surface imagery inspired by NASA's OSIRIS-REx and Viking Orbiters, featuring Valles Marineris and polar caps.
- **Mercury (Budh)**: High-resolution cratered surface imagery referencing NASA's MESSENGER orbital mission.
- **Jupiter (Guru)**: Jovian atmospheric cloud belt and Great Red Spot photography referencing NASA's Juno spacecraft and Hubble Space Telescope.
- **Venus (Shukra)**: Atmospheric true/enhanced color imagery inspired by NASA's Magellan and Pioneer Venus orbiters.
- **Saturn (Shani)**: Full ring system and atmospheric banding photography modeled on NASA/ESA/ASI Cassini-Huygens mission captures.
- **Rahu (North Node / Shadow Planet)**: Stylized cosmic eclipse and lunar node visualization. In Vedic Jyotish, Rahu is a mathematical/shadow point (Chhaya Graha); rendered with cohesive photographic quality.
- **Ketu (South Node / Shadow Planet)**: Stylized comet-nucleus and interstellar plasma visualization representing spiritual liberation and past-life karma (Chhaya Graha).
- **Ascendant (Lagna / Earth)**: Photorealistic planetary disc referencing NASA's iconic Apollo 17 "Blue Marble" view of Earth.

*All NASA space imagery is in the public domain in the United States.*
